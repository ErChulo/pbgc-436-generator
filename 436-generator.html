Welcome

This app builds the §436 Evaluation — the memo that decides whether any of the four IRC §436 funding-based limitations applied to the plan before DOPT, and how they affect the benefits PBGC calculates, values, and pays.

Workflow: Case → AFTAP history → Part 1 → Part 3 table → Review → Download.

The AFTAP history is the input. Type it in the grid, paste it from Excel, or let the scraper prompt propose it. The app computes the presumed AFTAPs, the periods, and the thresholds. It never certifies an AFTAP — PBGC will not calculate or certify a plan's AFTAP.

Help

AFTAP history

One row per plan year, from the later of the plan adoption date and PY2007. AFTAP % is the certified AFTAP, or the AFTAP stated in an AVR or Form 5500 Schedule SB where no certification exists. Certification date is the signed date. Source is Certification, AVR, or 5500 SB. IPS ref is the Image Viewer document number.

Presumed AFTAP

The prior year's certified AFTAP is presumed from the plan year start. If no certification by the first day of the 4th month, the presumed AFTAP drops 10 points from April 1. If none by the first day of the 10th month, the AFTAP is conclusively presumed below 60% from October 1.

Part 1

Answer the four questions. N/A covers the new-plan exception (plan in effect less than five full plan years) and the other listed exceptions. All No or N/A means no §436 limitation applied before DOPT.
